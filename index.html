Kuno Kurzhals , Ph.D.

I research visual analytics and eye tracking: developing visualizations of gaze data, evaluating how people interact with visualizations through user studies, and applying these methods in AR/VR, collaborative, and manufacturing settings. Current work focuses on multi-user eye tracking, LLMs as simulated users, and human–AI collaboration.
