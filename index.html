Kuno Kurzhals , Ph.D.

Junior Research Group Leader — Department of Visualization

My research is focused on video visualization and evaluation methods. I conduct user studies on the evaluation of visualization and video visualization techniques, with a special focus on the use of eye tracking for video-based evaluations. My work spans visual analytics, image-based visualization of gaze data, and the application of these methods in augmented and virtual reality as well as in collaborative and manufacturing settings.
